The 9-Phase Crowdfunding Model

Our platform implements the research-backed 9-phase model for movie and web series crowdfunding, ensuring success for all stakeholders.

1

Script Posting

Filmmakers post script abstracts and detailed project information, categorized by genre for easy discovery.

2

User Registration

Contributors register and are classified into A-star, B-star, and C-star tiers based on contribution levels.

3

Funding Phase

All-or-nothing funding model where projects must reach their goal to proceed to production.

4

Casting Phase

Democratic audition process where backers can participate in casting decisions and crew selection.

5

Decision Making

Collaborative decision-making between filmmakers and A-star contributors for key production choices.

6

Fund Transfer

Secure transfer of funds to filmmakers after successful funding and decision-making completion.

7

Distribution

Strategic distribution decisions including OTT platform selection and marketing strategies.

8

Revenue Generation

Revenue sharing and profit distribution based on the chosen crowdfunding model.

9

Promotion & Rewards

Reward distribution to backers and promotional activities for future project development.

Crowd Tier System

A-Star Crowd

>3% of Budget

  • Essential decision-making power
  • Cast and crew selection
  • Financial tracking access
  • Direct cast interaction
  • Audition participation

B-Star Crowd

1-3% of Budget

  • Suggestion rights
  • Limited audition access
  • Progress tracking
  • Credits inclusion
  • Virtual cast interaction

C-Star Crowd

<1% of Budget

  • Regular project updates
  • Signed merchandise
  • Basic rewards
  • Community access
  • Early content access

About CinemaFund

Academic Implementation

This platform represents a comprehensive implementation of the 9-phase crowdfunding model for movies and web series. The research identified the need for a standardized approach to film crowdfunding that benefits all stakeholders in the ecosystem.

Research Foundation

The platform is built on extensive research analyzing successful and unsuccessful crowdfunding campaigns in the film industry. Key findings include the importance of reward-based models, democratic decision-making processes, and the three-tier contributor classification system.

Key Features

  • 9-Phase Model: Structured approach from script posting to reward distribution
  • Democratic Participation: Contributors influence project decisions based on investment level
  • Transparent Process: Clear communication and progress tracking
  • All-or-Nothing Funding: Projects must reach their goal to proceed
  • Reward-Based System: Maintains filmmaker independence while engaging backers

Technology Stack

This implementation uses modern web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript to create a responsive, user-friendly interface. The single-file architecture ensures easy deployment and demonstration while maintaining professional standards.

Educational Purpose

This platform serves as an educational tool demonstrating the practical application of academic research in crowdfunding and film finance. It showcases how theoretical models can be translated into functional digital platforms that serve real-world needs.
